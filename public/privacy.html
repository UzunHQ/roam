Privacy and data

Short version: Roam does not know who you are, and your location never leaves your phone.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

What Roam never collects

  • Your location. GPS is used only inside your browser to draw the map and the route. It is never sent to any server, including ours.
  • Who you are. No accounts, no logins, no names, no student numbers, no email addresses.
  • What you type. The text you type in search stays on your phone.
  • Cookies or tracking across sites. Roam sets no cookies and has no adverts.

What Roam counts

Roam uses Umami Cloud (EU servers) to count, anonymously:

  • visits and pages opened,
  • which place was opened (for example "Library"),
  • that a search or a route was started (not what was typed),
  • device type and country, as reported by the browser.

Umami does not use cookies and does not store IP addresses. These numbers only show which parts of the map are useful.

Requests your phone makes for live information

  • Bus times: when you open a bus stop, your phone asks Transport for London (api.tfl.gov.uk) directly.
  • Library hours: your phone asks the university's own LibCal page (roehampton.libcal.com).

Like any website, these services see a normal web request. Roam adds nothing about you to them.

Stored on your phone only

Roam keeps a few settings in your browser (for example whether you closed the welcome card, and cached opening hours). You can clear them by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Security

  • HTTPS only, with a strict Content Security Policy: the browser is only allowed to talk to Roam, TfL, the university's LibCal and Umami.
  • No code from other websites runs in Roam. The analytics script is a pinned copy served from Roam itself.
  • The source code can be reviewed at github.com/UzunHQ/roam.
