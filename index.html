    Welcome to Roehampton

    Hello, explorer! Find your way around the University of Roehampton's Roehampton Lane campus.

    About Roam

    Roam is an independent student project. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, the University of Roehampton.

    Place details come from the university's own web pages and campus map, with the date each was checked. Opening hours change, so check the linked official page before you travel.

    Walking routes follow paths mapped on OpenStreetMap. Entrances have not all been mapped yet, so a route may end at the path beside a building.

    Your location stays on your phone. There are no accounts and no cookies.
